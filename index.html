The simulator generates loss as L = L₀ + k (θ − θ₀). When the Arduino is connected, the angle comes from the calibration fit in src/main.cpp instead.

Chrome or Edge only. Close the PlatformIO serial monitor first, since only one program can open the port. Connecting usually restarts the sketch, which vents for 5 s and re-zeros the optical and pressure baselines, so keep the finger straight.

Inflate runs the pump until you press Hold or Vent, or until the pressure limit set in src/main.cpp. Pressing the FSR vents straight away. If this page stops responding, the Arduino vents within 1.5 s and hands control back to the FSR.
