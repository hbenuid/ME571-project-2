The simulator generates loss as L = L₀ + k (θ − θ₀). When the Arduino is connected, the angle comes from the calibration fit in src/main.cpp instead. Make that fit on the Calibration page.

Chrome, Edge or Firefox 151+, not Safari. Close the PlatformIO serial monitor first, since only one program can open the port. Connecting usually restarts the sketch, which vents for 5 s and re-zeros the optical and pressure baselines, so keep the finger straight.

Inflate runs the pump until you press Hold or Vent, or until the pressure limit set in src/main.cpp. Pressing the FSR vents straight away. If this page stops responding, the Arduino vents within 1.5 s and hands control back to the FSR.

Records everything the Arduino sends, from Connect to Disconnect. The trial name goes in the file name and in every row.

  1. Connect with the finger straight and the bladder empty. The sketch zeros the optical loss as it starts.
  2. Capture 0° first. Then bend the finger to angles across the range you'll use, by hand or with Inflate and Hold.
  3. At each bend, measure the angle with a protractor, type it in and press Capture. Hold still while it averages.
  4. 6 to 10 points spread over the range is plenty. Capturing 0° again at the end shows whether the reading drifted.
  5. Copy the coefficients into src/main.cpp and upload.
